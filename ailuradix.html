One address for every world — from the Playmerth arcades to the speech therapy suite. Pick a door and step through.

What is Ailuradix?

Ailuradix is the front door — a single, permanent address that always knows where everything else lives. Instead of hunting through bookmarks or guessing a link, anyone lands here first and gets routed anywhere in one click.

The name is built to last: as new destinations get added, they show up on this page — the address itself never changes.

Purpose

Think of it less like a website and more like a front desk — one place whose only job is pointing visitors the rest of the way, so nothing gets lost and nothing needs to be remembered.

  • Water — heals +1 life
  • Springs — bounce you high
  • Moving platforms — time your jumps
  • Portals — teleport you
  • Drones — shoot lasers, dodge them
  • Flag — reach it to finish the level
  • A word popup mid-level asking you to read a word aloud, marked Correct/Incorrect
  • Save slots and a "Reset Progress" option on the start screen

Enemies march along a path from left to right — place towers on the grass to stop them before they reach the end.

  1. Pick a tower from the shop and click a grass tile to place it.
  2. Earn 🪙 coins from kills — spend them on 6 basic towers (Arrow, Cannon, Sniper, Freeze, Tesla, Flame).
  3. Earn 💎 gems from bosses and milestones — spend them on 8 stronger towers (Laser, Poison, Vortex, Meteor, Railgun, Time Warp, Storm, Plasma).
  4. Earn 🥇 gold rarely — spend it on upgraders placed next to a tower to boost it (place two of the same kind for a bigger bonus).
  5. Spend gems on power-ups like Nuke All or Freeze All when things get rough.
  6. Survive as many waves as you can — losing all your lives ends the run.

A top-down survival shooter — hold your ground and outlast wave after wave of enemies.

  1. Move with WASD or Arrow Keys (or drag to move on touch).
  2. Aim with the mouse and click to shoot — some characters auto-fire while held.
  3. Spend coins between waves on new characters (Gunner, Ninja, Phantom) and weapons (Flamethrower, Railgun, Minigun, Laser Beam, Shockwave, Sniper Rifle).
  4. Survive as long as possible — the longer you last, the more you earn.

A side-scrolling space shooter — blast alien ships, collect drops, and upgrade your ship between runs.

  1. Move freely in any direction with WASD or Arrow Keys.
  2. Your ship auto-fires at nearby enemies.
  3. Collect scrap and crystals dropped by destroyed enemies.
  4. Spend scrap on upgrades (Firepower, Fire Rate, Shield, Engine, Magnet, and more) and crystals on new weapons (Spread Shot, Plasma Lance).
  5. Trigger one-time power-ups like Overdrive for a temporary fire-rate boost.

An endless runner — time your jumps to keep going as long as possible.

  1. Press SPACE or UP to jump.
  2. Press DOWN to duck under obstacles.
  3. Press R to restart after a run ends.
  4. Spend the coins you earn on upgrades from the in-game shop.

A side-scrolling platformer — run, jump, and explore hand-built levels. Same engine as Shadow Dash, minus the speech-practice word popups.

  1. Move with Arrow Keys or WASD.
  2. Press SPACE (or Up/W) to jump.
  3. Collect coins, avoid spikes and lava, use springs and moving platforms, and reach the flag to clear each level.

Design your own alphabet, then translate text into it.

  1. Name your language and pick a separator character.
  2. In the Alphabet tab, assign a symbol to each letter — or switch to Full Create mode to draw your own symbols and pronunciations by hand.
  3. Switch to the Translator to type any text and see it instantly converted into your invented alphabet.

A multiplayer hacking game — protect your coins while trying to steal everyone else's.

  1. Set your own password to defend your stash.
  2. Pick a target from the hack list and try to guess their password.
  3. A correct guess lets you steal their coins; a wrong one fails the attempt.
  4. Spend earned coins upgrading your defenses in the shop.

An alchemy lab — combine elements to discover new ones.

  1. Click an ingredient from storage to load it into a workbench slot (double-click to fill both slots with the same one).
  2. Click Combine — a valid pair discovers a brand-new element; an invalid pair just fails.
  3. Keep discovering new elements to unlock rarer combinations.

A lemonade stand business sim — buy ingredients, set your price, and turn a profit before the day ends.

  1. Buy ingredients (lemons, sugar, ice, and more) each morning.
  2. Build a recipe and set your price with the slider — too high and customers walk away.
  3. Weather changes daily and affects sales — hot days move more lemonade.
  4. Review your profit at the end of each day, then start the next — unused ice melts overnight, so restock.

A fast-paced sushi restaurant sim — keep the customers fed before they walk out.

  1. Click ingredients to build a roll matching what a customer ordered.
  2. Drag the finished roll onto the customer (or into the bento box) to serve it.
  3. Work quickly — orders are timed, and happy customers mean bigger tips.

A kart racer with nitro boosts, power-ups, and a split-screen 2-player mode.

  1. Single player: steer/accelerate with WASD or Arrow Keys, SPACE for nitro, E or Shift for a power-up.
  2. 2-player split-screen: Player 1 uses WASD + Space (nitro) + E (power-up); Player 2 uses Arrow Keys + Enter (nitro) + / (power-up).
  3. Hit checkpoints in order to complete laps — finish all laps first to win.

Build and grow cities across islands, research new technology, and bridge the seas.

  1. Click empty land to place buildings that generate resources over time.
  2. Open the tech tree to research upgrades that boost production.
  3. Expand onto new islands and bridge them together as your empire grows.

A fruit-slicing arcade game — swipe your blade through flying fruit before it falls.

  1. Move the mouse (or swipe on touch) across the screen to slice anything it crosses.
  2. Avoid bombs — hitting one ends your run (unless you own the Lava Blade, which turns bombs into bonus points instead).
  3. Rocks can't be sliced — don't waste a swipe on them.
  4. Spend earned coins on blades with bonus effects (Golden for extra coins, Rainbow for random bonuses, Dragon for a burning trail, and more).

A Jeopardy-style math game show — pick a category and value, then answer before time runs out.

  1. Add players, then take turns picking a category and dollar value from the board.
  2. Answer correctly to add the points to your score — miss it and lose them instead.
  3. Watch for Daily Doubles, which let you wager your own points on one question.
  4. Whoever has the most points when the board is cleared wins.

A multiplayer math game — solve problems correctly to climb and stack your sushi tower.

  1. Take turns with the other player(s) — each turn shows a math problem to solve.
  2. Type the correct answer to add another piece to your stack and keep climbing.
  3. Whoever has the tallest completed stack at the end wins.

A daily brain-teaser hub with seven different puzzle types.

  1. Pick a puzzle type: Word Search, Word Ladder, Anagram, Cryptogram, Number Sequence, Word Guess, or Math Puzzle.
  2. Solve that day's puzzle — a new one generates daily.
  3. Revisit past puzzles from your saved puzzle list at any time.

A local 2-player arena chase — don't be the one who's "it" when time runs out.

  1. Player 1: Arrow Keys to move, Up to jump, / to boost, Right Shift to tag.
  2. Player 2: WASD to move, W to jump, Space to boost, Left Shift to tag.
  3. Tag the other player to pass "it" to them — avoid being it when the timer or point limit runs out.

A car-battle arena — smash, flip, and knock opponents around until you're the last one standing.

  1. Choose your arena size to start.
  2. Move with Arrow Keys or WASD, Up/W to jump, SPACE to brake.
  3. Ram opposing cars to flip or knock them around the arena.
  4. Press R to restart at any time.

Battlebound's sword fights meet Pulse Runner's speed — two platformer modes in one, with hazards, archery, and lives.

  1. Move with Arrow Keys or WASD.
  2. Press 1 or J to attack enemies.
  3. Normal mode is a 10-level speed-runner with hazards, buttons, and portals; Battle mode is Battlebound-style — sword attacks, sword blocks, archery, and upgrades.
  4. You start with 40 lives — use them wisely.

A terminal-style coding studio with its own mini programming language for building small games.

  1. Create or open a file from the terminal-style file list.
  2. Write code using the block library in the sidebar as reference, or drag blocks in directly.
  3. Press RUN to execute your code and see it play out on the canvas.
  4. Try Fight Mode to code a fighter — write punch, kick, and block logic to battle progressively tougher bots, unlocking new moves as you clear levels.

A tower-defense game where the path itself shifts on you.

  1. Place guns along the track to fire at enemies as they pass.
  2. Survive escalating waves of enemies.
  3. Every 10 levels the track changes — reposition your guns to cover the new path.

A Pac-Man-style maze chase — clear every dot while the monsters hunt you.

  1. Move with Arrow Keys or WASD.
  2. Collect every dot in the maze to advance to the next level.
  3. Grab the orange pellet to scare the monsters, the purple diamond to speed up, or the snowflake to freeze every monster in place.
  4. Avoid monsters when no power-up is active — getting caught costs a life.

Click to stop red blobs traveling down mountain trails before they reach the end.

  1. Click a red blob to eliminate it — spiders take a few clicks to bring down.
  2. Watch out for disguised blobs pretending to be safe (blue) — don't waste clicks on the wrong ones.
  3. Spend coins to deploy Rangers, who auto-attack blobs along the path for you.
  4. Reach the level's kill target to advance — there are 4 levels in all.

A city-builder — grow your population and income one building at a time.

  1. Place a House from Build → Housing to start growing your population.
  2. Tap any building and press Upgrade to boost its income and population by about 25% per level (up to 5 levels).
  3. Open Build → Working and place a Small Office — offices earn coins per population, every tick.
  4. Keep growing population with more houses so your offices earn more.
  5. Late game, unlock Transit (bus stops, train stations) for a big income jump — but build up population first.

A card game where you build number sequences into buildings and race an opponent to outscore them.

  1. Play cards from your hand that form an arithmetic sequence — the same difference between each card (like 3, 5, 7 or 5, 5, 5).
  2. A valid sequence builds a structure and scores points; an invalid one is rejected.
  3. Mix cards in certain ways to earn special action cards.
  4. Outscore your opponent's mini-city to win.

A classic block-puzzle — clear lines before you run out of room.

  1. Drag a piece from the tray onto the board.
  2. Fill an entire row or column to clear it and score points.
  3. Bigger pieces show up early — use them to clear lines fast.
  4. The game ends when none of the three pieces in your tray can be placed anywhere on the board.
