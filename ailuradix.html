Ailuradix

One address for every world — from the Playmerth arcades to the speech therapy suite. Pick a door and step through.

What is Ailuradix?

Ailuradix is the front door — a single, permanent address that always knows where everything else lives. Instead of hunting through bookmarks or guessing a link, anyone lands here first and gets routed anywhere in one click.

The name is built to last: as new destinations get added, they show up on this page — the address itself never changes.

Purpose

Think of it less like a website and more like a front desk — one place whose only job is pointing visitors the rest of the way, so nothing gets lost and nothing needs to be remembered.

What it is

The original playmerth.io collection — a growing library of free browser games spanning battle, platformers, creativity, strategy, math, and more. No installs, no ads.

Every game (18, plus 1 hidden)
  • Fortress Fury — build towers and defend against waves of enemies
  • Bullet Storm — survive waves of enemies in top-down combat
  • Cosmic Blitz — blast alien ships and upgrade your arsenal in a side-scrolling shooter
  • Sky Vaulter — time your jumps and reach the top
  • Platformer Adventure — run, jump, and explore side-scrolling levels
  • Shadow Dash — practice speech patterns while playing a platformer
  • Language Creator — design your own alphabet and translate text into it
  • Hackers — multiplayer: set your password, hack rivals, steal their coins
  • Mystic Forge — mix elements and discover new combinations
  • Lemon Tycoon — buy supplies, set prices, and chase profits
  • Sushi Surge — serve sushi to hungry customers fast
  • Nitro Blaze — race with nitro, power-ups, and split-screen 2-player mode
  • Auxotopia: Rise of Empires — build cities across islands and research tech
  • Slice Storm — slice flying fruit before it falls
  • Number Showdown — test your math skills, game-show style
  • Sushi Sensei — stack sushi by solving math problems
  • Brain Breaker — a fresh brain-teaser every day
  • Tag Frenzy — dodge and dash so you're never IT in this arena chase
  • Turbo Clash (hidden — see how-to) — smash, flip, and outlast in a car arena
Also on the page
  • Search bar and category filters (Battle, Platformers, Creativity, Strategy, Multiplayer, Math/Brainpower, Sushi Games, Special, Other, In Progress)
  • Day/night theme toggle and a background picker
  • A keyboard shortcuts panel and an About section
What it is

Playmerth 2.0 — the next-generation hub. Games load directly inside the hub instead of navigating away, alongside a live scene picker and theme controls.

Every game (7)
  • Platformer Rush — Battlebound sword fights meet Pulse Runner speed, hazards, archery, and lives
  • CodeLab — a terminal-style coding studio for creating and running small projects
  • Grove Guard — place guns, survive escalating waves, reposition as the track shifts every 10 levels
  • Monster Escape — maze chase: collect every dot while dodging monsters and grabbing powerups
  • Mongolia — click to stop red blobs on mountain trails, deploy rangers, spot impostors
  • Urbafier — place buildings, grow your population, expand your city grid
  • CityBuilder — build arithmetic sequences into buildings, mix cards, outscore opponents
Also on the page
  • A collapsible sidebar with the games list
  • A "Scene" picker with 20 real-world locations (English Countryside, Sahara Desert, Amazon Rainforest, and more)
  • Day/night and light/dark color toggles, plus a link back to Playmerth 1
What it is

The speech therapy practice hub. Therapists assign exercises and track weekly check-ins; clients log in to practice and earn coins they spend on game time.

Every unlockable game (8)
  • Fortress Fury — 1 coin = 5 waves, 3 coins = 20 waves, 5 coins = until you die
  • Sky Vaulter — 1 = one level, 3 = until you die, 5 = 10 lives
  • Platformer Adventure — 1 = one level, 3 = until you die, 5 = 10 lives
  • Mystic Forge — 1 = 5 combine tries, 3 = 5 new discoveries, 5 = unlimited
  • Lemon Tycoon — 1 = one level, 3 = until you die, 5 = 10 lives
  • Nitro Blaze — 1 = one level, 3 = until you die, 5 = 10 lives
  • Monster Escape — 1 = one level, 3 = until you die, 5 = 10 lives
  • Block Blast — 1 = one level, 3 = until you die, 5 = 10 lives
Also on the page
  • Separate login for clients and for the therapist
  • A client view: assigned exercise, weekly check-in calendar, coin count
  • A therapist view: add/remove clients, write exercises, review weekly progress
What it is

Shadow Dash — a side-scrolling platformer built around speech practice. Pick a word pattern and syllable count, then read words aloud as they pop up mid-level. (It shares a start screen with a second, unfinished mode called Platform Hop.)

Everything you'll run into
  • Coins — collect for points
  • Spikes — avoid, they hurt
  • Lava — stay away entirely
  • Water — heals +1 life
  • Springs — bounce you high
  • Moving platforms — time your jumps
  • Portals — teleport you
  • Drones — shoot lasers, dodge them
  • Flag — reach it to finish the level
  • A word popup mid-level asking you to read a word aloud, marked Correct/Incorrect
  • Save slots and a "Reset Progress" option on the start screen
  1. Type in the search bar — or press / to jump straight to it — to find a game by name.
  2. Click a category pill in the nav bar (Battle, Platformers, Creativity, Strategy, Multiplayer, Math/Brainpower, Sushi Games, Special, Other, In Progress) to filter the grid down to that genre.
  3. Click any game card to launch it full-page.
  4. Press Esc at any time while playing to return to the hub.
  5. Press T to flip between day and night themes, or G to open the background picker and choose a different scene.
  6. Click the ℹ icon in the nav to jump to the About section, or ⚙ to open background settings directly.
  7. Click Open Playmerth 2.0 → near the top of the page to switch hubs entirely.
  8. Turbo Clash is hidden as a "Forgotten Game" — look for the small secret trigger near the bottom of the page to reveal it.
  1. Click ⌂ (home) in the sidebar to jump back to Playmerth 1, or use the "Playmerth" text link right below the title.
  2. Click ☀ to toggle the day/night scene, and ○ to toggle light/dark colors for the whole hub.
  3. Open the Scene dropdown and pick one of 20 real-world locations (Sahara Desert, Amazon Rainforest, Swiss Alps, Maldives, and more) to change the background.
  4. Scroll the Games list below it and click a game's name to load it.
  5. The game loads directly into the frame on the right — no page change, no losing your place in the sidebar.
  6. Click the ‹ toggle at the top of the sidebar to collapse it and reclaim screen space for the game.
  1. On the landing screen, log in — clients use the username and password their therapist set for them; therapists use their own separate account.
  2. On the Client screen, read the exercise text your therapist wrote — it can include a specific practice time.
  3. Check off each part of that day's practice on the weekly calendar as you complete it — each newly checked box adds 1 coin (unchecking it removes the coin again).
  4. Your running coin total is shown at the top of the Client screen at all times.
  5. Open the Shop, pick a game, and choose a tier — cheaper tiers buy a shorter play (like one level), pricier tiers buy much more (until you die, or unlimited for some games).
  6. Therapists: log in to the Therapist view to add or remove clients, write or update each client's exercise, and review their weekly check-in history.
  1. On the start screen, type your name.
  2. Choose a Word Pattern to practice: Short Vowels, Vowel Teams, Magic E, Prefixes & Suffixes, Fake Bases + Affixes, or Latin & Greek Affixes.
  3. Choose a syllable count — 1, 2, or 3 syllables per word.
  4. Move with Arrow Keys or WASD; jump with SPACE (or Up/W) — or tap/swipe on a touch screen.
  5. Mid-level, a word will pop up — read it out loud, then click Correct or Incorrect based on how it went.
  6. Grab coins, avoid spikes and lava, bounce off springs, time your jumps on moving platforms, dodge drone lasers, and use portals to teleport.
  7. Step into water any time to heal +1 life.
  8. Reach the flag to finish the level.
  9. Use Reset Progress on the start screen if you ever want to wipe saved progress and start clean.
  10. On mobile: swipe to move, tap to jump.
