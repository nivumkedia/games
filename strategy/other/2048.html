How to Play

  • Swipe, or use arrow keys / WASD, to slide every tile at once.
  • Two tiles with the same number merge into one, doubling its value.
  • A new 2 or 4 tile appears after every move.
  • Reach the 2048 tile to win — keep going for a higher score after that.
  • Game ends when the board is full and no merge is possible.
