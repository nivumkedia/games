Tier 1: Blade Stance
how does this phase work?
Both fighters pick a move each round, revealed at the same time. Win a Level 1 clash to earn a Base Point. Bank 3 Base Points to fire a Level 2 move (costs the 3 points whether you win or not); winning gives a Level 2 Point. Bank 3 Level 2 Points to fire a Level 3 move; winning gives a Level 3 Point. Bank 3 Level 3 Points to Ascend to the next tier whenever you choose — ascending is permanent and resets your points. Higher tiers dominate lower tiers of equal or lower level; ties happen when the exact same move (or move-type) meets itself.

Both of you have mastered the 5 Pillars of Ninjutsu. For 4 rounds, pick an elemental card each round. Nobody wins or loses — instead the clash determines which Special Element token you collect. After 4 rounds, your most-collected element decides your Weapon Carrier.

how does this phase work?
Pick a weapon (each one locks on a 2-round cooldown after use), the universal Deflector, or — if you have a Master Token — a secret Final Blow / Healer. Ultimate Strikes crush Tactical & Base weapons; Tactical crushes Base; matching categories clash and tie. Deflector beats an Ultimate Strike (and earns you 3 points!) but fails against anything else, costing you a life. Score 5 points to earn a Master Token. Two Final Blow marks = instant death.
