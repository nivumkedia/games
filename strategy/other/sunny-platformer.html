How to Play

  • Arrow keys / A-D to move, Up / W / Space to jump.
  • On touch devices, use the on-screen arrows and jump button.
  • Walk straight up staircases — no jump needed for small steps.
  • On a ladder, hold Up/Down (W/S) to climb instead of jump.
  • Some spots let the path fork — pick a route, they meet up again later.
  • Hop across the grassy platforms and through tunnels — don't fall in the gaps.
  • Slides are a trust fall — step on one and you'll drop into a hidden area below. Totally optional.
  • Blue flags are checkpoints — touch one and you'll respawn there instead of the start.
  • Some checkpoints are a short detour off the main path — totally optional.
  • Reach the big red flag at the end to win!
