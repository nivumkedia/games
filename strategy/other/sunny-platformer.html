How to Play

  • Arrow keys / A-D to move, Up / W / Space to jump.
  • On touch devices, use the on-screen arrows and jump button.
  • Walk straight up staircases — no jump needed for small steps.
  • On a ladder, hold Up/Down (W/S) to climb — you'll stay locked to it, so side-to-side input won't knock you off.
  • Tunnels have a low ceiling — walk through them, there's no room to jump until you're out the other side.
  • Some spots let the path fork — pick a route, they meet up again later.
  • Bounce pads launch you automatically the moment you land on them — no jump button needed, and they reach much higher than a normal jump.
  • Trees, crystals, candy canes, and other landmarks are just scenery — they won't stop you or hurt you.
  • Slides are a trust fall — step on one and you'll drop into a hidden area below. Totally optional.
  • Blue flags are checkpoints — touch one and you'll respawn there instead of the start.
  • Some checkpoints are a short detour off the main path — totally optional.
  • Collect gold coins scattered along the way to spend in the shop.
  • Every map has its own layout, obstacles, and hidden areas — no two play the same.
  • Use ⌂ mid-level to head back to the map screen, or the ← arrow to leave the game entirely.
  • Reach the big red flag at the end to win!
